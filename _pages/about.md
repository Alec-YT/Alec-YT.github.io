# Alec Tristani
## Bio 
Hi! I'm a researcher passionate about numerical methods, particularly scientific machine learning, for underground construction and geomechanics. I was previously a postdoctoral researcher at Cornell University and I obtained my PhD from École Nationale des Ponts et Chaussées in France. I was recently awarded the 2025 Pierre Londe prize by the French committee of rock mechanics. **Currently on the job market!**

## Research 
My research aims to develop computational methods for simulating underground constructions, particularly tunnels. I focus on scientific machine learning for inverse and surrogate modeling, as well as on computational optimal experimental design to identify the most informative sensor placements for improving model calibration.

## Teaching and Tutorials
At Cornell University, I supervised a team of undergraduate students working on the design of utilidors — shallow tunnels in New York City intended to house utilities. The project involved traditional numerical modeling using finite difference methods and the implementation of machine learning surrogate models. 

In my free time, I write [hands-on tutorials](https://alec-yt.github.io/STAI-TUNED/) on applying scientific machine learning to tunnel design and underground construction.
